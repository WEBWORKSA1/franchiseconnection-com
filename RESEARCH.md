## Patterns every winner shares
1. **Three entry points to search**: industry, investment level, location. Plus lifestyle filters (home-based, semi-absentee, low cost, veteran, multi-unit).
2. **A low-commitment quiz** before the high-commitment form (Entrepreneur, VettedBiz, franchiseportal.de, BizBuySell).
3. **Multi-brand request basket** — the core lead-gen mechanic of Franchise Direct / Gator / Opportunities.
4. **Two-step lead form**: email first, then phone + capital. Capital/net-worth fields are what make a lead sellable.
5. **Audience routing**: buyer vs franchisor vs supplier/advisor.
6. **Trust**: methodology pages, editorial standards, "cannot pay to be ranked", disclosures, association badges.
7. **Money tools**: net worth, fundability, funding calculators.
8. **Fresh content**: news feed, rankings, events calendar with countdowns, video library.
9. **Monetization stack**: pay-per-lead, featured placements, sponsored content, directories, events, memberships.

## Gaps FranchiseConnection.com exploits
- Most portals are pay-to-play brand lists with thin education. We lead with **independent education + calculators** (AdSense-friendly, high-CPC finance keywords) and convert with a **free matching report**.
- Nobody pairs an **affordability-first budget dial** with category economics (typical investment, royalty, staffing, owner role) for brand-neutral comparison.
- Few cover **US + Canada + UK/AU** rules on one page (FTC Rule, provincial acts, Franchising Code).
