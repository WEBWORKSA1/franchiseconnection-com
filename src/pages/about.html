About FranchiseConnection.com

We help people make one of the largest financial decisions of their lives with better numbers and fewer sales pitches.

Why we exist

Most franchise websites are directories paid for by the brands they list. That model is useful — franchisors need buyers — but it means the first thing a curious buyer usually meets is a sales pitch. We think buyers deserve a brand-neutral first step: what different kinds of franchise really cost, how they make money, what the owner actually does every day, and how to check a franchisor's claims.

What we do

  • Category research. We profile 75 franchise categories using typical ranges from published Franchise Disclosure Documents, with the main risk to check for each.
  • Calculators. Affordability, total investment, SBA loan payments, break-even, lifetime royalty cost and multi-unit cash needs.
  • Guides. Long-form explainers on buying, financing, disclosure law and franchising your own business.
  • Matching. A free match report that ranks categories against your budget and goals, with introductions to franchisors, consultants or lenders only when you ask.
  • News and events. A daily-refreshed headline feed and an expo calendar.

How we make money

We are a commercial website. We earn from display advertising (Google AdSense), from franchisors who pay for featured placement or qualified buyer leads, from sponsors, and from optional reader support. Paid placements are always labelled. Payment never changes our category data, calculator logic or guide conclusions. Read the full editorial policy.

What we are not

We are not a franchisor, a franchise seller, a lender, a law firm or an investment adviser. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell a franchise or a recommendation to buy one. Always review a brand's current FDD with a qualified franchise attorney and accountant.
