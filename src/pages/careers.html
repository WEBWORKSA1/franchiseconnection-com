Help people buy the right franchise

We're a small, remote-first team building the most useful independent franchise resource on the web. We work with freelancers, partners and part-timers across the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.

  • Franchise writer (freelance)

    Remote, per article

    Write and update buyer guides, category explainers and franchisee stories. You know franchising — as an owner, consultant, lender, attorney or journalist — and can explain it plainly.

  • Video creator (freelance)

    Remote, per project

    Script, film and edit short explainers for YouTube and social: calculator walkthroughs, FDD item breakdowns, owner interviews.

  • Franchise consultant partner

    Commission / referral

    Independent franchise consultants who help our buyers who ask for personal guidance. Transparent about how you are paid.

  • Partnership sales (part-time)

    Remote, commission + base

    Sell featured listings, lead programs and sponsorships to franchisors, lenders and suppliers.

  • Fact-checker and data researcher

    Remote, hourly

    Review FDD filings and public sources to keep category ranges, laws and lending rules current.

  • Campus ambassador

    Students, stipend

    Bring franchise education to entrepreneurship clubs and business schools.

How we work

  • Remote-first, asynchronous, outcome-focused.
  • Paid on time, every time. Clear briefs and rates agreed up front.
  • Editorial independence: we never publish earnings claims outside Item 19.
  • Credit for your work, and a portfolio that franchise brands see.
