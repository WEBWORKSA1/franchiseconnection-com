Official rules (summary)

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not improve your chances.

  1. Sponsor: FranchiseConnection.com.
  2. Eligibility: open to individuals aged 18 or older (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction) who are legal residents of the United States (excluding where prohibited), Canada (excluding Quebec), the United Kingdom and Australia. Employees of the sponsor and their immediate families are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  3. Entry periods: the Pitch Challenge runs each calendar quarter and closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the last day of the quarter. The Franchise IQ draw runs each calendar month and closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the last day of the month. The countdowns above show the current periods.
  4. How to enter: complete the relevant form on this page. Limit one IQ entry per person per month and one Pitch Challenge entry per person per quarter.
  5. Winner selection: Pitch Challenge winners are chosen by judges using the published criteria. The IQ winner is drawn at random from all eligible entries; quiz answers do not affect the odds. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries.
  6. Prizes: Pitch Challenge: US$1,000 (or equivalent) plus a featured story; runner-up: a one-hour plan review. Franchise IQ: one US$100 (or equivalent) digital gift card. Prizes are not transferable. Canadian winners must correctly answer a time-limited skill-testing question.
  7. Notification: winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be selected. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  8. Publicity: with consent, winners' first names, locations and entries may be published.
  9. Privacy: entry information is handled under our privacy policy.
  10. General: the sponsor may cancel or modify a contest if it cannot run as planned, and may disqualify entries that are fraudulent, offensive or infringe others' rights.
