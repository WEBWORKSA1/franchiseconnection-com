How to work a franchise expo as a buyer

  1. Arrive with your numbers. Know your liquid capital, net worth and preferred owner role — run the affordability check first.
  2. Shortlist booths in advance. Use the exhibitor list to pick 8–12 brands in two or three categories.
  3. Ask the same five questions at every booth: total investment range, how many units opened and closed last year, whether there's an Item 19, whether your territory is available, and what support looks like in the first 90 days.
  4. Attend the seminars. The financing and legal sessions are often the most valuable hour of the day.
  5. Don't sign or pay anything at the show. In the US, you must receive the FDD at least 14 calendar days before signing or paying.
