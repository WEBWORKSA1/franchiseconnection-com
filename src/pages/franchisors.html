Franchisor FAQ

Where do your buyers come from?

Organic search on franchise education and calculator topics, the monthly Market Pulse newsletter, social video and partner referrals. Buyers find us while researching, before most brands contact them.

Do you sell the same lead to many brands?

Pay-per-lead buyers are shared with no more than three brands that match their criteria, and only when the buyer has asked for introductions. Every brand sees the same capital and timeline fields.

Can we cap monthly spend?

Yes. Set a monthly lead cap and capital, territory and timeline filters. You are never billed beyond the cap.

Do you work with franchise consultants and lenders too?

Yes. Consultant networks and SBA lenders can join our partner program — contact us through the advertise page.
