How we score fit

Budget fit carries the most weight: we assume lenders finance about 70–75% of the project, so your liquid capital supports roughly three times its value in total investment. Industry interest, owner role and your nice-to-haves add points. Scores are a starting point for research, not a recommendation to buy.
