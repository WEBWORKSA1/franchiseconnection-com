Franchise resources

Official sources every franchise buyer should bookmark: regulators, free FDD databases, lending programs, industry associations and free mentoring. All links open the original organisation's website.

US regulators and rules

  • FTC Franchise Rule — the federal disclosure rule (16 CFR Part 436).
  • FTC: A Consumer's Guide to Buying a Franchise
  • 16 CFR Part 436 full text (eCFR)
  • NASAA — the association of state securities and franchise regulators.

Free FDD databases

  • California DFPI regulated-entity search — franchise registrations and filings.
  • Wisconsin DFI franchise search
  • Minnesota Department of Commerce — CARDS holds ten years of franchise registration documents.
  • Use several years of filings to see how fees, Item 19 and closures have changed.

Lending and government help

  • SBA 7(a) loans
  • SBA 504 loans
  • SBA Franchise Directory — brand eligibility list for lenders.
  • SCORE — free mentoring from experienced business owners.
  • America's Small Business Development Centers
  • Canada Small Business Financing Program

Industry associations

  • International Franchise Association (US)
  • Canadian Franchise Association
  • British Franchise Association
  • Franchise Council of Australia
  • ACCC: Franchising Code of Conduct (Australia)

Professional help to budget for

A franchise attorney to review the FDD and agreement, an accountant to stress-test projections and structure the business, and a commercial real estate broker or lawyer for the lease. Ask each for a fixed quote before starting.

Franchise consultants

Consultants (also called brokers) help you shortlist brands and are typically paid a commission by the franchisor when you buy. Ask how they are paid, which brands they represent, and whether they will show you brands outside their portfolio.
